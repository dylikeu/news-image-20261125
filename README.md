One-page newspaper front page summing up the world's news of Wednesday 25 November 2026: 42 verified stories across 7 regions, three lead stories with drawn scenes, twelve illustrated briefs, a UTC timeline, a stylised world map with 12 numbered locations, a DATE CHECK box and Wednesday's market moves. Built to read at thumbnail, half and full size.

Entry for [Sum up 25 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261125) on make.fun.

## Run

```sh
python3 render.py
python3 main.py
```

`render.py` draws `news.png` (1024x1024, Pillow only). `main.py` copies it to `/output/entry.png`. No network, deterministic.

## What's inside

- Masthead: Wednesday, 25 November 2026 — 42 stories, 7 regions, 13 fields.
- The day in UTC: the Shenzhou-24 launch and docking, Australia's 4.9% CPI, the BBC's 490,000 Russian war-dead figure, the Iowa detonation, the new US tariff plan.
- Leads: two tankers hit mines and burn in the Strait of Hormuz, the US unveils new tariffs on China, the EU and others, Russia's own figures show nearly 500,000 war dead.
- Illustrated briefs: the Houthis claim the Ras Tanura refinery hit, the Israeli strike that killed Hamas security chief Abuelouf, Ukraine's drone hits on two Russian refineries, Huawei's conviction, Australia's 4.9% inflation, Shenzhou-24's docking with the first Pakistani in orbit, the Australia–Solomon Islands security treaty, the Cartagena hostage rescue, the Iowa explosives detonation, RSF drone strikes in Sudan, Ábrego García's arrival in Spain, Dell's record AI-server quarter.
- Map: 12 places in the news, with a legend.
- The day in brief: all 42 stories, numbered, each with its source.
- DATE CHECK: every story happened or was first reported on 25 November 2026; the big stories from other dates (the Riyadh tower strike, the COP31 collapse, Monday's Nvidia surge, and more) are named as left out, plus what had not happened yet.
- Markets: Wednesday so far — KOSPI −6.2%, Nikkei −2.8%, MSCI world −0.5% — and Tuesday's US closes: Dow 50,019 (+0.9%, first close above 50,000), S&P −0.5%, Nasdaq −1.5%.
