## Lead stories

1. **Two tankers hit mines and burn in Strait of Hormuz.** Two oil
   tankers struck naval mines, exploded and caught fire in the Strait
   of Hormuz early on Wednesday, Iran's Revolutionary Guards Navy said
   in a statement carried by state media; the tankers were on a route
   Iran calls unauthorised. Reuters, 25 Nov 2026 (DUBAI). Tag
   REPORTED: the claim comes from the IRGC and had no independent
   confirmation in the Reuters report.
2. **US unveils new tariffs on China, EU and others.** The Trump
   administration announced new tariff proposals on a host of
   countries including China, as the USTR prepared findings from a
   probe into excess industrial capacity in 16 major trading
   partners. BBC, 25 Nov 2026 (21:19); Reuters, 25 Nov 2026.
3. **Russia's own figures show nearly 500,000 war dead.** At least
   490,334 Russian soldiers have died since the start of the war in
   Ukraine in 2022, according to leaked Russian government statistics
   analysed by BBC News. BBC, 25 Nov 2026.

## Medium stories

4. **Houthis claim Ras Tanura refinery hit.** Yemen's Houthis said
   they attacked Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura oil refinery, the
   kingdom's largest, and Riyadh's financial district in three
   phases; the Saudi-led coalition said air defences intercepted four
   drones over Ras Tanura, and NASA satellite imagery showed thick
   black smoke over the refinery. Reuters, 25 Nov 2026.
5. **Israeli strike kills Hamas security chief.** Mohammad Abuelouf,
   a Hamas colonel who heads the militants' interior security
   service, was killed with his wife and two children in an
   airstrike on their Gaza City apartment early on Wednesday — the
   second Israeli attack to kill a senior Hamas security official in
   24 hours. Reuters, 25 Nov 2026 (CAIRO).
6. **Ukraine drones hit two Russian refineries.** Ukrainian drones
   reportedly hit two Rosneft refineries — Syzran and Kuibyshev —
   in Russia's Samara region overnight, setting both ablaze; Ukraine
   also said five Russian military jets were hit in a strike on the
   Savasleyka airfield. BBC, 25 Nov 2026.
7. **Huawei convicted of stealing US trade secrets.** A New York
   jury found the Chinese firm guilty of conspiracy to steal trade
   secrets and bank fraud, but acquitted it of the overarching
   racketeering charge. BBC, 25 Nov 2026 (10:59). Note: the verdict
   was delivered late Tue 24 Nov in the US; BBC's report and the
   wide coverage are dated Wed 25 Nov.
8. **Australia inflation jumps to 4.9%.** Consumer prices climbed
   by more than expected in October as fuel and clothing jumped,
   though the Reserve Bank is expected to hold rates. Reuters,
   25 Nov 2026.
9. **Shenzhou-24 docks; first Pakistani in orbit.** China launched
   Shenzhou-24 at 00:18 UTC; it docked with Tiangong at 03:56 UTC
   and the crew entered the station at 06:35 UTC. Pakistani
   astronaut Muhammad Zeeshan Ali is the first Pakistani in orbit
   and the first foreigner aboard Tiangong. Reuters / Xinhua,
   25 Nov 2026.
10. **Australia, Solomons sign security treaty.** The Solomon
    Islands and Australia signed a treaty in Canberra that would
    preclude a foreign military or security base on the Solomon
    Islands, ending the Solomons' controversial security pact with
    China. BBC / Just Security, 25 Nov 2026.
11. **Police free 20 hostages in Cartagena siege.** Colombian
    police stormed a supermarket in Cartagena to free 20 people
    held hostage for 17 hours; all hostages were freed and one
    captor was killed. BBC, 25 Nov 2026 (17:56).
12. **Army detonates 5 tons of explosives in Iowa.** The US Army
    carried out a controlled detonation of nearly five tons of
    unstable lead azide at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant in
    Middletown at 9:00 a.m. CST (15:00 UTC); residents nearby were
    told to stay indoors. NPR / army.mil, 25 Nov 2026.
13. **RSF drone strikes kill 19 civilians in Sudan.** Sudanese
    human rights group Emergency Lawyers said drone strikes by the
    paramilitary Rapid Support Forces killed 19 civilians and
    wounded 20 others in two cities. Just Security, 25 Nov 2026.
14. **Ábrego García arrives in Spain.** Kilmar Ábrego García, the
    Salvadoran man at the centre of the Trump deportation fight,
    arrived in Spain after voluntarily leaving the US, ending a
    20-month legal battle. BBC, 25 Nov 2026.
15. **Dell posts record quarter on AI servers.** Dell reported
    record Q3 results: revenue $54.9bn, up 103% year-on-year; AI
    server revenue $22.0bn, up 291%. Reuters, 25 Nov 2026
    (earnings reported after the Tue close; reported Wed 25 Nov).
